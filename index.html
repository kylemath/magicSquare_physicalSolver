Magic squares as spectral height fields

A normal magic square is a constrained permutation of heights over a lattice. Its centered 3D covariance is almost too forgiving, so this paper studies multiscale energy spectra that expose local structure.

Formal model

From mass balance to spectral geometry

Centered coordinates X_j = 2j-(n-1), Y_i = 2i-(n-1)
Centered height Z_ij = 2a_ij-(n²+1)
Magic constraints Σ_row Z = Σ_col Z = Σ_diag Z = 0
Energy spectrum H_p(k) = -Σ_W P_p(W) log P_p(W)

The ordinary 3D covariance is fixed by the value set and line constraints. The geometric signal moves into higher-order and multiscale quantities, especially local energy distributions E_p(W)=Σ|Z|^p across all windows of scale k.
